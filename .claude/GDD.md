# GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT: Project LondPunk

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
- **Elevator Pitch**: A turn based CRPG, based off of cyberpunk, but in a different universe and set in London.
- **Core Pillars**: 
  - Story Rich Narative
  - Character customisation and progression
  - Medium sized areas with zones to split them up for exploration.
  - Detailed locations with great exploration and character building.
- **Target Audience**: Fans of *Baldur's Gate 3*, *Warhammer 40k: Rogue Trader* and *Cyberpunk 2077*.

## 2. Core Game Loop
1. **Explore**: Explore the world find secrets, fight enemies in turn based battles.
2. **Upgrade**: Upgrade abilities and gear on the character.
3. **Customisation**: Customise your character.
4. **Fight or Talk**: Fight or Talk your way out of battle.

## 3. Key Systems & Mechanics
### Combat & Movement
- Free-Moving Camera with Tilt, Rotate and zoome across the environment using your mouse or controller. (Whilst out of combat)
- Turn-based movement similar to *X-Com 2* and *Baldur's Gate 3* (In Combat)

### Meta-Progression & Economy
- Experience points to level the character
- Reputation with the different factions
- Class based perks and upgrades
- Clothing/Gear and cyberware/augments
- Money

## 4. Aesthetic & Narrative Scope
- **Visual Style**: Cyberpunk 3D Design, London architecture with Blade runner aeshetic.
- **Tone**: Gritty, industrial, desperate.

## 5. Current MVP Scope (Focus Areas)
- [] Basic player movement
- [] Single class with following stats
    INT (Intelligence): problem-solving, memory, awareness
    REF (Reflexes): ranged combat and driving/piloting
    DEX (Dexterity): melee combat, athletics, evasion
    TECH (Technique): building, repairing, using tools
    COOL: willpower under pressure, intimidation, staying calm
    WILL (Willpower): determination and grit, and it factors into your Hit Points
    LUCK: a pool of points you spend to boost rolls, which refreshes each session
    MOVE (Movement): how far you can move in a turn
    BODY: strength, toughness, endurance
    EMP (Empathy): relating to people, and it sets your Humanity
- [] Single enemy type which has the same stats as the player.
